About Me

I am a PhD student in Higher Education Leadership at Indiana State University, with a professional background in I-O psychology, organizational behavior, and applied research.

My interests sit at the intersection of human behavior, organizations, higher education, technology, leadership, and the changing nature of work. This website is my personal space to share research, public writing, books, media, conferences, and the ideas and experiences that shape my work.
