About Me

My work begins with a question: how can workplaces and educational institutions help people do meaningful work, continue learning, and thrive through change?

I am a PhD student in Higher Education Leadership at Indiana State University, with a background in industrial-organizational psychology, organizational behavior, and applied research. My professional journey across psychology, consulting, and research has shaped a practical, people-centered perspective on leadership and development.

I connect psychological theory with real organizational and educational challenges, with particular interest in how leadership, culture, and technology influence people’s experiences and opportunities.
