ABOUT

Connecting psychology, organizations, and higher education.

I am Mohammad Daei Jafari, a PhD student in Higher Education Leadership at Indiana State University. My academic and professional interests sit at the intersection of organizational behavior, I-O psychology, technology, leadership, employee well-being, and higher education.

My work draws on a background in psychology and management and focuses on practical, evidence-informed approaches to organizational and workforce development.

On this website, I share my research, publications, professional experience, academic interests, and selected projects.
