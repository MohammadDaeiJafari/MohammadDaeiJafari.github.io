About Me

People, Work, and Learning

My work begins with a question: how can workplaces and educational institutions help people do meaningful work, continue learning, and thrive through change?

I am a PhD student in Higher Education Leadership at Indiana State University, with a professional background in industrial-organizational psychology, organizational behavior, and applied research. My academic interests connect the psychology of work with the leadership and learning systems that shape people’s opportunities.

I am especially interested in the relationship between leadership, employee development, meaningful work, resilience, and emerging technologies. I aim to bring a people-centered perspective to organizational and educational challenges, connecting theory and evidence with questions that matter in practice.
