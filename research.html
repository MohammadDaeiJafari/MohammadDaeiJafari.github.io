Scholarship & Projects

Research

Journal articles, manuscripts under review, and conference presentations across organizational psychology, leadership, employee well-being, and higher education.

Research Themes

My research examines how people experience work and learning, how organizations support development, and how emerging technologies shape leadership and institutional life.

Meaningful Work & Employee Well-Being

Meaning, resilience, engagement, and psychological experiences that help people sustain their work.

Leadership & Employee Development

Leadership practices, learning climates, workforce development, and opportunities for growth.

AI & Organizational Behavior

Implications of artificial intelligence and workplace technology for employees, teams, and organizations.

Higher Education Leadership

Institutional effectiveness, student experience, international education, and organizational change in higher education.
